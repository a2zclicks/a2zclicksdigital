Our process

How we actually work

No onboarding calls that go nowhere. Here's exactly what happens after you reach out.

01
We listen first

Before we touch a single graphic or line of code, we spend time understanding your business — what you sell, who your customers are, and what's not working right now.

02
We look at your competition

We dig into what your competitors are doing online — their content, their SEO, their ads — and find the gaps we can use to your advantage.

03
We build the plan

Based on what we learn, we put together a content strategy tailored to your industry — what to post, when to post it, what format works best, and what story to tell.

04
We execute and manage

Our team creates the reels, designs the graphics, writes the captions, and posts everything on schedule. You review, approve, and we handle the rest — consistently, every month.
